Employer journey

Make recruitment easier through European mobility

Already written your job offer? Import a PDF or add the link to your advert. Match Europe helps you search for candidates in Europe.

Import my job offer

Your offer is read, then checked with you

Match Europe reads and analyses your document to turn its information into a structured summary: role, skills, languages and working conditions.

Check this summary, correct any information if needed and complete what is missing.

If information is missing or unclear, you will be asked additional questions. In particular, specify whether you can welcome cross-border workers or people relocating from another European country.

Choose questions to prequalify applications against job requirements, such as availability, experience, languages or working hours. Interested candidates answer before an introduction. Their answers help you identify applications to pursue and reject those that do not meet job requirements. No candidate is rejected automatically solely on the basis of their answers.

The search for candidates starts only after you validate your offer.

How the service can help

Broaden your search beyond your local market, prepare candidate prequalification and talk to interested candidates, with support from your EURES adviser.

What happens next

  • 1. Add your offer as a PDF, a link or manual text.
  • 2. Check the information and answer any additional questions.
  • 3. Choose your questions for candidates, then validate your offer.
  • 4. Review applications and answers before deciding on an introduction.

You stay in control

A match between an offer and a profile remains a suggestion. An introduction requires both your agreement and the candidate’s. Your adviser then starts a three-way conversation.

Accessible support

  • On desktop and mobile.
  • Targeted questions with quick replies and free text.
  • An EURES adviser to support the conversation.
