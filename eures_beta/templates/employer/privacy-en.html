Information about your personal data

How Match Europe uses your information

Match Europe is an EURES network experiment conducted with France Travail. This notice describes the processing currently planned for questionnaires, matching and the limited WhatsApp Business test.

Version dated 3 August 2026. France Travail is responsible for the processing carried out as part of the Match Europe experiment. Eric Barthélémy is the sole contact point during this phase.

Company contact in the local trial

The contact name and email, plus the optional phone and role, are stored with the local draft so the adviser can contact you. The employer can view and edit them in this browser. The assigned adviser and administrators can view them in their workspaces. These fields are not shown to candidates, added to offer data sent to AGLAE or included in the offer export. Retention and the production notice remain to be defined; this historical copy does not validate the new journey.

01

Why do we use your data?

  • Understand a professional mobility project or recruitment need.
  • Identify possible matches between candidates and employers.
  • Offer and monitor a human-supervised introduction.
  • Assess the experiment using aggregated indicators.
02

What information is involved?

  • Answers to the candidate or employer questionnaire.
  • Contact details needed to contact you again.
  • Professional information relevant to matching.
  • Evidence of your choices, contact dates and the outcome of an introduction.
03

Who can access it?

  • Authorised members of the Match Europe team and, where needed for an introduction, the relevant EURES partners.
  • An employer or candidate receives only the information needed for the proposed introduction.
  • Personal data is not published; public statistics are aggregated.
04

AI-assisted offer preparation

  • Artificial intelligence helps structure your offer into a summary for review. Results may contain errors or omissions; you can correct them before confirming.
  • Offer information is sent to a technical AI preparation provider. This journey does not send your contact email to that provider. Avoid including personal contact details or sensitive data in the offer.
  • This step does not decide whether to accept or reject an application. Recruitment and introduction decisions remain human.
05

Candidate prequalification

  • The employer defines questions related to job requirements, such as availability, skills, languages or working hours. Interested candidates answer before a possible introduction, using suggested answers and/or free text.
  • Answers help check essential job requirements and help the employer and adviser decide which applications to pursue or reject. No candidate is rejected automatically solely on the basis of their answers.
  • Draft answers remain private to the candidate. Once submitted, answers are available to the employer concerned and the authorised adviser supporting the application.
  • Questions must relate to professional job requirements. Do not include health data, identity documents or other sensitive information in free-text answers.
  • Submitting answers does not constitute agreement to open a conversation. An introduction requires separate agreement from both the candidate and employer, followed by the adviser’s intervention.
06

Voluntary participation

  • You may refuse to participate or ask for contact to stop.
  • Refusing WhatsApp does not prevent the use of another channel such as email or telephone.
  • Match Europe does not make automated recruitment decisions.
07

Limited WhatsApp Business test

  • WhatsApp is offered only after a match has been identified and after separate agreement from the candidate and employer.
  • In a group, other participants can see your number, name or username, profile picture depending on your settings, and the messages you post.
  • Do not share identity documents, health data, passwords or other sensitive information in the group.
  • Match Europe records evidence of agreement and the matching outcome in EURES beta but does not copy the WhatsApp conversation.
  • WhatsApp is a Meta service governed by its own terms and data processing. Messages may remain on participants’ devices until deleted.
08

Retention and security

  • Operational data is retained for 12 months after the last contact, then deleted or anonymised when no longer needed.
  • Access is limited to authorised staff and professional accounts use strengthened authentication.
  • A data incident or misuse will suspend the relevant test.
09

Your rights and contact

  • You may request access, rectification or deletion, withdraw your agreement or object to certain uses where the law allows.
  • Eric Barthélémy is the sole contact point during this phase. To exercise your rights or ask a question: matcheurope.00040@francetravail.fr.
  • You may also lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority.
