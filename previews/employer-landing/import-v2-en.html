Your offer is saved. To participate in the trial and receive proposals, enable notifications for your Match Europe space.

Receive proposals and messages

Notifications are required to participate in the trial. They can alert you when the page is closed, depending on your device settings. On some devices, you must first install Match Europe on your home screen.

If you decline or cannot enable them, your offer stays saved, but trial participation is not finalised. You can return to this step later.

What happens next

  1. We search for profiles compatible with your offer with the help of artificial intelligence.
  2. For shortlisted candidates, we check availability, interest and continued acceptance of the proposed conditions.
  3. After review by an adviser and agreement from both parties, a discussion dedicated to this offer opens in your Match Europe space, with the candidate and adviser.

Local trial: notification activation and trial enrolment are not connected yet. No matching, prequalification or introductions are started.
